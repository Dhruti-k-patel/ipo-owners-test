IPO Owner's Test

Twenty-one questions from the OWNERS framework. Answer each from the red herring prospectus, not from memory. Five are gates: a “No” on any of them ends the test. A “Don't know” anywhere sends you back to the document.

Three ways to get the figures
  1. Pick an IPO from the list. The calculator fills itself from figures compiled from the offer document, with page references.
  2. Find the offer document yourself. Search the web for the RHP · SEBI filings · NSE IPOs · BSE IPOs. Use the RHP (red herring prospectus) if it exists; the DRHP is the earlier draft and its figures may be older.
  3. Type the figures. Paste the document link below so it stays with your answers, then fill the calculator from the pages it names.
