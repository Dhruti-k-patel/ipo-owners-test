IPO Owner's Test

Twenty-one questions from the OWNERS framework. Answer each from the red herring prospectus, not from memory. Five are gates: a “No” on any of them ends the test. A “Don't know” anywhere sends you back to the document.

How to get the figures
  1. Find the offer document. Search the web for the RHP · SEBI filings · NSE IPOs · BSE IPOs. Use the RHP (red herring prospectus) if it exists; the DRHP is the earlier draft and its figures may be older.
  2. Fill in the values. Open the PDF alongside this page. Under every box in the calculator is the heading to search for inside the PDF with Ctrl+F; click it to copy. The page does not read the document for you.
Show where to look in any RHP

Page numbers differ from one document to the next, so use the table of contents on the first pages, or press Ctrl+F (⌘F on a Mac) in the PDF and search for the heading. Click a search term to copy it. Printed page numbers usually run a few pages behind the PDF viewer's page counter.
